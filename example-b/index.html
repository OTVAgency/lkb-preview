About Lori

A career built around helping people find their best self.

I began my career working in community houses with special needs adults, then in primary and alternative education with at-risk youth while completing my Bachelor’s in Social Work. I transitioned to post-secondary education with special needs students to complete my Master’s in Social Work. Other experiences include crisis, mental health skill building, case management, intensive in-home, therapeutic day treatment, and outpatient.

Time has opened many doors for me to help others find their best self through grace, hope, and self-love.
