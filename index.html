Who she is

Lori Kier-Benson, LCSW

LKB Counseling LLC · Licensed in Virginia

I began my career working in community houses with special needs adults, then in primary and alternative education with at-risk youth while completing my Bachelor’s in Social Work. I transitioned to post-secondary education with special needs students to complete my Master’s in Social Work. I also have more than 12 years with crisis work, mental health, continuing skill building, case management, intensive in-home, and therapeutic day treatment.
